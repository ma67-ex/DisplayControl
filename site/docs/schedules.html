Schedules

Triggers

GroupTriggers
TimeA time of day on chosen weekdays (none chosen means every day).
SunSunrise or sunset, with an offset of up to four hours before or after.
SystemMac wakes, screen locks, screen unlocks, DisplayControl starts, Dark or Light Mode turns on.
PowerPlugged in, or on battery.
MonitorsAny or a specific monitor connects or disconnects.

Create a preset first, then add a schedule in Settings, Schedules. Each can be switched on and off without deleting it.

When the condition ends

For paired triggers (plugged in and on battery, Dark and Light Mode, lock and unlock, monitor connects and disconnects) you can pick a second preset to apply when the condition ends.

Sunrise and sunset

Allow your location once, or type latitude and longitude. The times are calculated on your Mac with the NOAA solar equations, accurate to about a minute or two. In polar day and polar night there is no sunrise or sunset, and those schedules skip until there is.

Sleep, clock and daylight saving

  • Timers are one-shot and computed for the next fire time, so nothing wakes the Mac while idle.
  • If the Mac slept through a time-based schedule, the most recent missed one runs once when it wakes, if it was within two hours.
  • Timers are recomputed after wake and when the clock or time zone changes. Wall-clock times survive daylight saving changes.
  • Starting the app is not treated as a monitor connecting, so a "monitor connects" schedule does not fire at launch.
