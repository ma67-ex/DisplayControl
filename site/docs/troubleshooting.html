Troubleshooting

A monitor does not respond

  1. Switch the monitor's picture mode to Custom. Game, Movie and Reader modes often lock brightness and contrast.
  2. Look for a DDC/CI option in the monitor's menu and turn it on.
  3. Connect the monitor directly, without a hub, dock, KVM or adapter, to see whether that was the problem.
  4. Try another port. If HDMI fails on your Apple Silicon Mac, use USB-C or DisplayPort.
  5. In the display's expanded card, press Re-detect, or choose a control mode by hand.
  6. Run displaycontrol probe and displaycontrol selftest "name".

Reading probe: External candidates: 0 means macOS exposes no DDC service for that monitor. read failed after a successful pairing means the monitor did not give a valid answer.

A screen stays dim

Run displaycontrol reset-gamma, or choose Reset dimming in the window. If the app is not running, relaunch it first, or log out and in.

The keyboard keys do nothing

  • Is the setting on, and is DisplayControl ticked in System Settings, Privacy and Security, Accessibility?
  • Rebuilt the app? Remove it from the list and add it again.
  • The keys only apply to the display under the pointer. Move the pointer onto the monitor.

The icon is missing from the menu bar

Too many menu bar items can hide it. Quit a few, or use a menu bar manager and make sure DisplayControl is shown.

Sunrise schedules do nothing

They need a location. Add one in Settings, Schedules.

Report a problem

Open About, choose Copy diagnostics, and paste the result into an issue with your monitor and Mac models. The diagnostics contain no personal data: macOS version, chip, display vendor and product IDs, control modes and recent DDC log lines.
