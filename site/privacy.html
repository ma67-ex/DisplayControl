Privacy

DisplayControl collects nothing.

  • No network access. The app has no networking code. The "Check for updates" button opens a web page in your browser; the app itself never makes the request.
  • No analytics, no crash reporting, no accounts.
  • Stored on your Mac only: display settings, presets, schedules and preferences, as JSON in ~/Library/Application Support/DisplayControl/.
  • Location is optional, requested only when you choose it for sunrise and sunset schedules, used to compute those times on your Mac and saved there as two numbers. You can type coordinates instead.
  • Accessibility is optional and used only to read the brightness and volume keys.
  • Diagnostics stay on your Mac until you choose to copy them.

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts or fonts.
